Everything it does

Six small rituals,
one calm hour.

01

Set the hour

A round dial sits inside a moonlit landscape. Drag the lamp-gold knob anywhere from 10 to 120 minutes; the arc follows your finger and the number settles in the middle.

  • Intention. Write what this hour is for, “Rotational motion”, “Chapter 4”, and it travels with you into the session.
  • Subjects. Tag the session as Physics, Chemistry, Maths or Reading so your stats sort themselves.
  • Streak. A small “day run” pill counts how many days in a row you showed up.
02

Choose your sound

Five soundscapes, each with its own volume, so you can build the room you study best in: Rain, Café, Brown noise, Forest and Fireplace. Tap a chip on the home screen to switch one on, or open the mixer to blend them.

  • Mixer. Five sliders and a Listen button to preview before you start.
  • Record ambience. Capture up to three minutes of your own sound: a fan, a window, a quiet library. It joins the mixer as a sixth track.
03

Step into a scene

Start, and the app falls away. The whole screen becomes a night sky with hills, mist and a moon: a big serif countdown, a thin lamp-gold progress line, and a short quote that changes every minute.

  • Three scenes. Moonlit Hills, Ember Ridge and Rain Window, or your own photo taken in-app.
  • A plant that grows. A sapling at the foot of the hills puts out leaves as the session runs.
  • Screen stays awake for the whole session, and nothing else competes for your attention.
04

Stay with it

Hush gives you a couple of quiet reasons not to wander off.

  • Flip to focus. Place the phone face-down on the desk to start a session, or to resume a paused one.
  • Let the plant keep you here. Turn this on and leaving the app early wilts the plant. Off by default.
  • Pause or end with two soft glass buttons. Ending asks once, kindly, before it lets you go.
05

See it add up

Every finished session lands in your stats. Nothing to set up, nothing to log by hand.

  • Week, Month, Year. One big total, a bar chart, and how you compare with the period before.
  • A season of focus. A 12-week heatmap, one square per day, deeper green for longer sessions.
  • By subject and your garden: one plant for every session you completed.
06

A gentle nudge

Set it once and let it look after itself.

  • Daily reminder at the time you choose: a soft invitation, never a nag.
  • Default duration in five-minute steps, so the dial opens where you like it.
  • Home-screen widget showing today’s focus minutes, one tap from the app.

Installing takes a minute

  1. Download the APK on your phone. If the browser warns that the file could be harmful, choose Download anyway.
  2. Open it from the download notification, or from Files → Downloads.
  3. Allow the install. Android asks once: tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for your browser or file manager, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open. That’s it.
Good to know
  • Done installing? You can switch the permission off again under Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.
  • To update, install the newer APK over the old one. Your stats and settings stay. Uninstalling removes them.
  • Play Protect may show a one-time “unrecognised app” notice, because Hush isn’t on the Play Store. Choose Install anyway or More details → Install.
  • In a few countries Android now also asks for its newer “advanced” install flow for apps outside an app store. Follow the on-screen steps if you see it.
